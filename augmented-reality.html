Augmented Reality Experience

A historical storytelling experience combining illustration, animation and Augmented Reality to create an engaging digital exhibition.

Overview

This project explored the integration of animation and augmented reality. The objective was to transform static graphic elements into interactive digital experiences by combining motion graphics with AR technology.

Role

Motion Design
Animation
Augmented Reality

Software

Adobe Photoshop
Adobe After Effects

The Challenge

The challenge was to create engaging animations that would seamlessly integrate into an augmented reality environment while maintaining visual quality and usability.

The Solution

The illustrations were first designed in Adobe Photoshop and then animated using Adobe After Effects. Finally, the animations were imported into EyeJack Creator, allowing users to experience the project through augmented reality on mobile devices.
