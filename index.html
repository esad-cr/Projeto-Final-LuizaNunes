My Skills

Graphic Design

Graphic Design is the art of communicating ideas through visual elements such as typography, colors, images, and layouts. It is used in branding, advertising, posters, websites, and social media to create attractive and effective communication. A good graphic designer combines creativity with technical skills to deliver clear visual messages.

Mulimédia Design

Multimedia Design combines different types of media such as text, video, audio, animation, and graphics into interactive experiences. It is widely used in websites, video games, digital marketing, and mobile applications. Multimedia designers focus on both creativity and user experience to create engaging digital content.

Processing Design

Processing Design is related to creative coding and generative art using programming to create visual and interactive projects. Designers use code to develop animations, digital installations, and experimental graphics that react to user interaction or data. It connects technology, art, and design in innovative and dynamic ways.
