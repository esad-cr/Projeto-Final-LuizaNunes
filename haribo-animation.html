Haribo Gummies Animation

A 3D animation inspired by the iconic Haribo Gummy Bears, exploring modelling, animation, lighting and rendering in Blender.

Overview

This project consisted of creating a short 3D animation inspired by Haribo Gummies. It explored modelling, materials, lighting, animation and rendering techniques using Blender to produce a playful and visually engaging result.

Role

UI Design
Brand Identity
Website Design

Software

Figma
Adobe Illustrator

The Challenge

The animation was created in Blender, from scene composition and materials to camera animation and final rendering, resulting in a colourful and dynamic promotional-style video.

The Solution

Using Adobe Illustrator and Figma, I developed the logo, colour palette, typography system and complete website interface. The final result presents a refined digital experience focused on clarity, elegance and usability.
