About Me

Hello, I'm Luiza Nunes

I am a Design and Multimedia student passionate about creativity, digital communication, and innovative visual experiences. My academic journey has allowed me to explore different areas of design, from graphic design and branding to web design and multimedia production.

I enjoy combining creativity with technology to create meaningful projects that engage and inspire people. Through my studies, I have developed skills in visual communication, digital content creation, web development, image editing, video production, and interactive media.

I believe that good design goes beyond aesthetics. It is about solving problems, telling stories, and creating experiences that connect with audiences. Every project is an opportunity to learn, experiment, and improve my abilities.

In addition to my academic work, I am always looking for new challenges that allow me to expand my knowledge and explore emerging technologies in the creative industry.

This portfolio represents my growth as a designer and multimedia creator, showcasing projects that reflect my dedication, creativity, and passion for digital innovation.
